Structured Lifecycle

Registration & Attendance Lifecycle

StratosEvent implements four operational stages to guarantee capacity integrity and prevent attendance duplication.

Stage 01

Registered Initiated

Participant submits an application. A composite unique key (user_id, event_id) enforces zero duplicate submissions.

Stage 02

Pending Approval Verification

Transactional lock checks remaining seat capacity against the event limit before authorizing reservation.

Stage 03

Approved Ticket Issued

Participant receives a verified pass with a unique reference code (e.g., TKT-STR-2026-0001) for QR check-in.

Stage 04

Checked-in Admitted

Event staff scans the pass on event day. The unique attendance key strictly prevents multiple check-ins of the same ticket.

Core Capabilities

Enterprise-Grade Architecture

Engineered with concurrency protection, transactional seat locks, and secure role separation.

Role-Based Access Control

Strict separation between Participants (discovery and ticketing) and Administrators (event moderation and check-in auditing).

Duplicate Registration Lock

Database-level composite constraint (user_id, event_id) guarantees a user cannot hold redundant reservations for the same session.

Capacity & Concurrency Guard

Pessimistic row locking in MySQL transactions guarantees that concurrent registrations never exceed the maximum seat threshold.

Unique Digital Pass Codes

Each reservation receives a cryptographically safe, human-readable identifier ready for QR and barcode scanning at venue entry.

Single Check-In Verification

Attendance records are constrained to unique registration references, completely closing the door to pass reuse or attendance fraud.

Audit & Attendance Reporting

Real-time visibility into capacity utilization, arrival timestamps, and attendance status logs for post-event evaluations.
